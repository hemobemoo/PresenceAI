# S1 rubric anchors: draft for review

Rubric `s1_presentation` version 0.1, status **draft**. Thresholds: **proposed**, decided in ADR 0017.

> Generated by `uv run python scripts/render_rubric_review.py` from `resources/rubrics/s1_presentation.yaml` and `configs/rubric.yaml`. Do not edit by hand.

## How to read this draft

- Five dimensions follow the AAC&U VALUE Oral Communication rubric, each with four levels: 4 Capstone, 3 Milestone 3, 2 Milestone 2, 1 Benchmark.
- Every anchor describes **observable behaviour with a number**. A level is reached when all of its conditions hold; the dimension gets the highest level reached; level 1 is the default.
- A measure that cannot be computed (camera off, no time limit, Arabic content) is left out of the conditions and never counts against the speaker. A dimension with no measurable condition is reported as not measurable.
- Skill score for the adaptive engine: y = (level − 1) / 3, so 0, 0.33, 0.67, 1.
- **Every number is proposed.** Numbers from the plan (FINAL_04) are the opening 20 %, the closing 15 % and the first 2 sentences; all others are first proposals for a 2–3 minute talk.
- "Left out" lists the parts of the VALUE wording that no rule can observe. They are not scored by rules.

## Central message

VALUE dimension: Central Message. Feeds the skills: `central_message`.

| Level | Anchor (observable) | Conditions |
|---|---|---|
| 4 Capstone | A sentence stating the main point appears within the first 20 % of the words, is stated again within the last 15 %, and its key phrases recur in at least 3 sentences of the body. | `text.central_message.in_opening` = yes<br>`text.central_message.in_closing` = yes<br>`text.central_message.key_phrase_recurrences` ≥ 3 (`cm_recurrences_min`) |
| 3 Milestone 3 | A sentence stating the main point appears within the first 20 % of the words and is stated again within the last 15 %. | `text.central_message.in_opening` = yes<br>`text.central_message.in_closing` = yes |
| 2 Milestone 2 | The main point is stated explicitly in at least 1 sentence, but not both in the opening and in the closing. | `text.central_message.statements` ≥ 1 (`cm_statements_min`) |
| 1 Benchmark | No sentence states the main point; it can only be inferred from the content. | default |

Left out (not observable by rules): compelling, memorable; consistent with the supporting material (needs a content check; judge slice).

## Organization

VALUE dimension: Organization. Feeds the skills: `organization`, `opening_conclusion`.

| Level | Anchor (observable) | Conditions |
|---|---|---|
| 4 Capstone | The talk has an introduction cue in the first 20 % and a conclusion cue in the last 15 %, at least 4 transitions between parts, and an opening hook (question, statistic, story, quotation or surprising claim) in the first 2 sentences. | `text.organization.framing_parts` ≥ 2 (`org_framing_full`)<br>`text.organization.transitions` ≥ 4 (`org_transitions_high`)<br>`text.hook.present` = yes |
| 3 Milestone 3 | The talk has both an introduction cue and a conclusion cue, and at least 2 transitions between parts. | `text.organization.framing_parts` ≥ 2 (`org_framing_full`)<br>`text.organization.transitions` ≥ 2 (`org_transitions_mid`) |
| 2 Milestone 2 | The talk has at least 1 of the two: an introduction cue or a conclusion cue. | `text.organization.framing_parts` ≥ 1 (`org_framing_min`) |
| 1 Benchmark | No introduction cue and no conclusion cue are found. | default |

Left out (not observable by rules): skilful; makes the content cohesive; purposeful choice of pattern (chronological, problem-solution).

## Language

VALUE dimension: Language. Feeds the skills: `language_clarity`.

| Level | Anchor (observable) | Conditions |
|---|---|---|
| 4 Capstone | No rare term (word frequency below 3 on the Zipf scale) is used without a definition, and sentences average at most 20 words. | `text.language.undefined_terms` ≤ 0 (`lang_undefined_max_4`)<br>`text.language.mean_sentence_words` ≤ 20 (`lang_sentence_max_4`) |
| 3 Milestone 3 | At most 1 rare term is used without a definition, and sentences average at most 25 words. | `text.language.undefined_terms` ≤ 1 (`lang_undefined_max_3`)<br>`text.language.mean_sentence_words` ≤ 25 (`lang_sentence_max_3`) |
| 2 Milestone 2 | At most 3 rare terms are used without a definition, and sentences average at most 30 words. | `text.language.undefined_terms` ≤ 3 (`lang_undefined_max_2`)<br>`text.language.mean_sentence_words` ≤ 30 (`lang_sentence_max_2`) |
| 1 Benchmark | More rare terms are used without a definition, or sentences are longer on average. | default |

Left out (not observable by rules): imaginative, memorable, compelling; appropriate to the audience; free from bias (no rule can observe these).

## Supporting material

VALUE dimension: Supporting Material. Feeds the skills: `supporting_material`.

| Level | Anchor (observable) | Conditions |
|---|---|---|
| 4 Capstone | At least 5 supporting items of at least 3 different types (statistics, examples, analogies, quotations). | `text.support.items` ≥ 5 (`sup_items_high`)<br>`text.support.types` ≥ 3 (`sup_types_high`) |
| 3 Milestone 3 | At least 3 supporting items of at least 2 different types. | `text.support.items` ≥ 3 (`sup_items_mid`)<br>`text.support.types` ≥ 2 (`sup_types_mid`) |
| 2 Milestone 2 | At least 1 supporting item. | `text.support.items` ≥ 1 (`sup_items_min`) |
| 1 Benchmark | No statistic, example, analogy or quotation is found. | default |

Left out (not observable by rules): credibility and relevance of the sources; how strongly the material supports the presentation (judge slice).

## Delivery

VALUE dimension: Delivery. Feeds the skills: `fluency`, `pacing`, `audience_gaze`, `concision_timing`.

| Level | Anchor (observable) | Conditions |
|---|---|---|
| 4 Capstone | At most 2 fillers per 100 words, at most 1 mid-sentence pause of 1 s or more per minute, gaze toward the camera for at least 70% of speaking time (camera on), and no time over the limit. | `fillers.per_100_words` ≤ 2 (`del_fillers_4`)<br>`pauses.mid_sentence_per_min` ≤ 1 (`del_mid_pauses_4`)<br>`vision.gaze.at_camera_share` ≥ 0.7 (`del_gaze_4`)<br>`timing.overrun_s` ≤ 0 (`del_overrun_4`) |
| 3 Milestone 3 | At most 4 fillers per 100 words, at most 2 mid-sentence pauses of 1 s or more per minute, gaze toward the camera for at least 50% of speaking time (camera on), and at most 15 s over the limit. | `fillers.per_100_words` ≤ 4 (`del_fillers_3`)<br>`pauses.mid_sentence_per_min` ≤ 2 (`del_mid_pauses_3`)<br>`vision.gaze.at_camera_share` ≥ 0.5 (`del_gaze_3`)<br>`timing.overrun_s` ≤ 15 (`del_overrun_3`) |
| 2 Milestone 2 | At most 8 fillers per 100 words, at most 4 mid-sentence pauses of 1 s or more per minute, gaze toward the camera for at least 30% of speaking time (camera on), and at most 30 s over the limit. | `fillers.per_100_words` ≤ 8 (`del_fillers_2`)<br>`pauses.mid_sentence_per_min` ≤ 4 (`del_mid_pauses_2`)<br>`vision.gaze.at_camera_share` ≥ 0.3 (`del_gaze_2`)<br>`timing.overrun_s` ≤ 30 (`del_overrun_2`) |
| 1 Benchmark | More fillers, more mid-sentence pauses, less gaze toward the camera or more time over the limit than level 2. | default |

Left out (not observable by rules): posture and gesture (camera; P6 measures have no thresholds yet, shown as information); vocal expressiveness (pitch and loudness variation are opt-in only, ADR 0013); speech rate (judged only against the speaker's own band, never an absolute norm; P5); the VALUE wording about how the speaker appears (an inference about the person, which the app never makes; AGENTS.md rule 3).

## Measures

| Measure | Status | Needs | Languages | Description |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `text.central_message.statements` | planned (P2-b) | none | en | Number of sentences with a thesis cue ("today I will", "my main point is"). |
| `text.central_message.in_opening` | planned (P2-b) | none | en | Yes if a thesis-cue sentence falls within the opening share of the words. |
| `text.central_message.in_closing` | planned (P2-b) | none | en | Yes if a sentence within the closing share of the words repeats the key noun phrases of the opening thesis sentence. |
| `text.central_message.key_phrase_recurrences` | planned (P2-b) | none | en | Body sentences that repeat a key noun phrase of the thesis sentence. |
| `text.organization.framing_parts` | planned (P2-b) | none | en | 0-2: an introduction cue (purpose or preview) in the opening share, and a conclusion cue ("in conclusion", "to sum up") in the closing share. |
| `text.organization.transitions` | planned (P2-b) | none | en | Transition and sequence cues between parts ("first", "next", "finally"). |
| `text.hook.present` | planned (P2-b) | none | en | Yes if one of the first sentences is a question, a statistic, a story opening, a quotation or a surprising claim (pattern rules). |
| `text.support.items` | planned (P2-b) | none | en | Supporting items found (statistics, examples, analogies, quotations). |
| `text.support.types` | planned (P2-b) | none | en | Distinct types among the supporting items (0-4). |
| `text.language.undefined_terms` | planned (P2-b) | none | en | Rare terms (word frequency below the rare-word threshold) used without a definition cue ("which means", "that is") in the same or the next sentence. |
| `text.language.mean_sentence_words` | planned (P2-b) | none | en | Mean words per sentence. Depends on the transcript's punctuation; not measurable when the punctuation is unreliable (ADR 0011). |
| `fillers.per_100_words` | available | none | en, ar | Hesitations per 100 words (speech/fillers.py). With base Whisper this is a lower bound and Arabic is low confidence (ADR 0012). |
| `pauses.mid_sentence_per_min` | planned (P2-c) | none | en | Pauses of at least 1 s inside a sentence, per minute of speaking time (from the pause events of speech/pauses.py; Arabic positions are unknown, ADR 0011). |
| `vision.gaze.at_camera_share` | planned (P6) | camera | en, ar | Share of speaking time with gaze zone at_camera (ADR 0004). |
| `timing.overrun_s` | planned (P3) | session_limit | en, ar | Seconds spoken beyond the session's time limit (0 when within it). |

## Questions for the reviewer

1. Do the anchors keep the meaning of each VALUE dimension, given what is left out?
2. Are the proposed numbers reasonable for a 2–3 minute student presentation?
3. Should any left-out part be judged by a language model with a quoted transcript span (only with the user's consent, ADR 0017)?
